# Force users

Answers task-graph node `or-q-mod-force-dqed` (epic `or-ep-mod-surface-vdch`), from the Feature Wiki page `executable/mod-force`; its detection, discovery and creation claims were reread here. Sides are the game's: 1 Alliance, 2 Empire. "DAT" means a column of MJCHARSD/MNCHARSD (major and minor characters), "GNPRTB n" a General Parameter id (all eight columns of every id quoted here hold the same value, so none varies by side or difficulty). `hyp:` marks anything not traced through code.

## Answer

1. **Force is three shorts and three flag bits; no tier field exists.** `entity[9]>>6&3` (`+0x24` bits 6-7) is the object's owner (1 Alliance, 2 Empire, 3 Neutral: `side-numbering.md`): `FUN_0055e4d0` takes it as its first argument and never reads it, and `FUN_00560a30` passes it as the side filter of its character walk. `entity[0x1e]&1` (`+0x78` bit 0) is the mission decoy flag (`decoy-roll.md`).
2. **A character is a Force user when `+0x8c` is non-zero.** `+0x8c = +0x8e + +0x90` (`FUN_004eee40`). `+0x8e` is the base level, `+0x90` the Jedi Training bonus. Three flag bits in `+0xac` gate it: bit 5 "sensitive", rolled once at creation against the DAT `jedi_probability`; bit 4 "aware", copied from the DAT `is_known_jedi` at creation and set later by discovery; bit 7 "discoverer". The base level `+0x8e` is only filled in when bits 4 and 5 are both set (DAT `jedi_level_base + rand(0..variance)`). All of that is DAT-driven and side-blind.
3. **Teacher and trainee are DAT-driven, with a numeric floor, and open to both sides.** A teacher needs the DAT `is_jedi_trainer` flag and `+0x8c >= GNPRTB 2581` (100). The shipped data gives that flag to Luke (`0x32000242`) and Vader (`0x35000281`) only, not to Palpatine (`0x34000280`). Each trainee needs `+0x8c != 0` and a level strictly below the teacher's; the team needs two or more members; the mission's side must be 1 or 2. Nothing in the five Jedi Training functions reads a character id or the mission side beyond that. MISSNSD has `IsAlliance = 1` and `IsEmpire = 1` for `0x58000042`.
4. **The Empire can train and can discover.** Vader starts at Force 120 with the trainer flag, so he is a valid teacher at game start and he runs the discovery timer (`FUN_00560a30`, needs Force >= GNPRTB 2580 = 80) for Imperial characters at his location. The Empire's 27 sensitive minor characters (DAT probability 10 or 20) have `+0x8c` 0 until discovered, then 10 to 70. What the Empire lacks is only a reason to do it: the computer player never starts Jedi Training (`FUN_00402720` compares the mission id with `0x58000042` and sets no capability bit), so only a human Empire player can. The Alliance's teacher, Luke, starts at 50 and is below the teacher floor of 100 until his Force rises, mostly through story events.
5. **Detection of Force users on missions is symmetric and keys on Luke and Vader by id.** In the detection phase of every mission with detection phases, `FUN_0058a9e0` and `FUN_0058ab00` flag Luke `0x32000242` and Vader `0x35000281` if they are in the manager's lists, plus one random entry per list; for each pair `FUN_0058a3f0` rolls `chance = Force(a) + Force(b) + GNPRTB 3587 (-100)` percent, both Forces non-zero. A hit marks the mission detected (manager `+0x4c` = 1) unless the member is a decoy, and (`FUN_0055ff60`) reveals both characters and pairs them. No side test appears in these functions.
6. **Vader and Palpatine get, by id and not by DAT: (a)** the role of mentor in the Luke/Leia meeting reward (`FUN_00560200`: ids `0x35000281` and `0x34000280` pay out Force `+0x8e` to Luke and Leia only; neither villain ever gains Force from it); **(b)** for Vader, a slot in the detection hook above (Palpatine has none); **(c)** in the AI's object flags, `FUN_00402cf0` treats only Luke and Vader as "Force >= 100" (`0x200`), so Palpatine at 150 gets neither the `0x100` nor the `0x200` class bit; **(d)** the story chains (`story-missions.md`). Palpatine has no `is_jedi_trainer`, so he cannot teach and, lacking bit 7 eligibility via that flag, never discovers anyone. No Dark Side attribute or branch exists in the code read here: "Dark Side" is only high Force on an Empire character plus these ids.

## Evidence

**Fields.** The character constructor `FUN_004eceb0` zeroes `+0x8c`, `+0x8e` and `+0x90` and leaves `+0xac` untouched by name (`FUN_004eceb0.c`). `FUN_004ee0d0`, `FUN_004ee150` and `FUN_004ee1d0` are the three setters; each clamps to 0..0x7fff, writes the short, and calls the vtable slot `+0x31c`, `+0x320` and `+0x324` of the base character vtable `0x0065ca70`. Slot `+0x31c` is `FUN_004f16a0` (skills, then `FUN_004ef070`); `+0x320` and `+0x324` are `FUN_004f1700`, a thunk to `FUN_004eee40`, so `+0x8c` always equals `+0x8e + +0x90`. `FUN_004ee9c0`, `FUN_004ee950`, `FUN_004eeaa0` and `FUN_004ee8e0` call `FUN_0053a640` with masks `0x20`, `0x10`, `0x80` and `0x04` on `+0xac`. Slots `+0x358` and `+0x35c` are `FUN_004f1b30`, a thunk to `FUN_004eee60`; slot `+0x364` is `FUN_004f1b70`, which arms timer `0x389` (`FUN_0053fa60(0x389, ...)`).

**Events.** `FUN_004f1e00`, `FUN_004f1e50` and `FUN_004f1ea0` post `0x1e1`, `0x1e4` and `0x1e5` (`FUN_0053fcf0`). They sit at `+0x31c`, `+0x320` and `+0x324` of nine parallel tables (for example `0x00665c58`), next to a table that overrides `+0x31c` with `FUN_0056fb80` for Luke (`0x00665fd8`). That `0x1e1` follows `+0x8c`, `0x1e4` `+0x8e` and `0x1e5` `+0x90` is therefore hyp: it rests on the slot numbers matching, not on a traced call from the setter to the dispatcher. The ForceAware, ForcePotential and DiscoveringForceUser dispatchers (`FUN_004f2240`, `FUN_004f2280`, `FUN_004f2300`) sit at `+0x358`, `+0x35c` and `+0x364` in the same tables, which fits bits 4, 5 and 7.

**Creation.** `FUN_004f11f0` reads the character's class record through `+0x2c`: `+0x9c` into `FUN_004ee950` (aware), `+0x98` into `FUN_0053e2f0` (the roll: `FUN_0053e2e0` is `FUN_0053e290(DAT_00661a88 - 1)`, and `DAT_00661a88` is the constant 100, `build-delivery.md`), then `FUN_004ee9c0`, `FUN_004eee60`, `FUN_004eee40`, and the skill refreshers. `FUN_004eee60` reads class `+0xa0` and `+0xa4`. The mapping of those class offsets to the DAT columns `jedi_probability`, `is_known_jedi`, `jedi_level_base`/`variance` and `is_jedi_trainer` (`+0xb8`) is by order and role: the class record is the file record shifted by `0x28` (`jedi_level_base` at file offset `0x78` is `+0xa0`, `is_jedi_trainer` at `0x90` is `+0xb8`), and the reads fit (Luke and Vader are the two trainers).

**Skills.** `FUN_004eecb0` writes effective diplomacy as `base(+0x58) + base × (+0x8c) / 100` (`FUN_0053e940` is `FUN_0053e190(base, force) + base`, and `FUN_0053e190(a, b)` is `a × b / DAT_00661a88`); `FUN_004eecf0` does espionage from `+0x5a`; `FUN_004eed90` combat from `+0x62` minus injury `+0x94` (`FUN_0053e900`). Leadership (`FUN_004eede0`) uses `+0x92`, not Force. Vader at 120 therefore has 2.2 times his base diplomacy, espionage and combat. hyp: which effective skill each offset feeds (`+0x7c` to `+0x8a`) is read from `decoy-roll.md`, not re-traced here.

**Detection.** `FUN_0055e4d0(tier, a, b, &hit, &chance)`: when `a` and `b` are both non-zero, `chance = a + b + DAT_006bb718`; `DAT_006bb718` is loaded from GNPRTB `0xe03` (3587, shipped -100) by `FUN_0055e340`; when `chance > 0`, `hit = FUN_0053e2f0(chance)`. `FUN_0058a3f0` calls it with the first character's `+0x8c` (`entity[0x23]`, the short at `0x8c`) and the second's. On a hit it sets the manager's `+0x4c` to 1 when the first character's `+0x78` bit 0 (decoy) is clear, then, when `FUN_00506e80` is non-zero, calls `FUN_0055ff60` (both characters must have no `+0x9c` partner; it sets each one's `+0x9c` to the other and reveals both through `FUN_004f9510` and, for a character in a system, the system at level 6, hyp: the argument 6 of `FUN_0050d5a0` is the observation level). `FUN_00506e80` returns the pointer at game root `+0xc0` (id `0xfa00000a`, created by `FUN_00519910`), so the gate is that object's existence; timer `0x389` (`FUN_0057cff0`) has the same gate. The caller chain is `FUN_00589970` → `FUN_00589e40` → `FUN_0058a2c0` → `FUN_0058a3f0` (`decoy-roll.md` lists `FUN_00589e40` as untraced). `FUN_0058a9e0` and `FUN_0058ab00` build the walk: Luke `0x32000242`, then Vader `0x35000281`, then one random unflagged entry per list (`FUN_0058aca0`), each flagged in the manager's lists (`FUN_0058ac50`). That the manager lists are the mission's two sides is hyp: they are at `+0x50`/`+0x58` and `+0x60`/`+0x68`.

**Discovery.** `FUN_00560a30` (timer `0x389`, gated by `FUN_00506e80`) requires the firing character's `+0xac` bit 7, walks the same-owner characters at its location (`FUN_004f2640` with the owner bits `+0x24 >> 6 & 3`), skips prisoners (`+0xac` bit 0) and mission members (`+0x78` bit 7) and any with bit 4 already set or bit 5 clear, and for Leia `0x31000241` requires the discoverer to be Luke `0x32000242` with `+0xb0` bit 0 (`FUN_0056fe90` sets Leia's). Then `FUN_004ee950` (bit 4) and `FUN_004ee680` (`+0xa4` := discoverer, event `0x362`). Bit 4 triggers slot `+0x358`, which runs `FUN_004eee60`, which gives the new Force user his level. The timer's period is not traced.

**Jedi Training.** `FUN_00570690` runs the shared checks (`FUN_005868c0`), then in instance mode (`+8` set; lists at checker `+0x24`/`+0x28`/`+0x2c`) `FUN_00570940`, in class mode `FUN_00570bc0` (lists `+0x18`/`+0x1c`/`+0x20`, DAT `+0xa0 + +0xa4` for Force, class `+0xb8` for the trainer flag). Instance mode: `FUN_00570e40` walks the first list for a family `0x30`-`0x3b` member whose class trainer flag (`FUN_004ed220`) is set and whose `+0x8c >= DAT_006b90dc` (GNPRTB `0xa15` = 2581, shipped 100); none → `0x40/0x90`; if one is found but the mission side (`checker +0x10`) is not 1 or 2 the check fails without a code. Each other member with `+0x8c == 0` → `0x40/5`; a member of another id with `+0x8c >= teacher` → `0x40/0xd`; a team of one → `0x40/0x94`. The chance (`FUN_005710b0`) is `FUN_0055c7c0`: teacher minus student `+0x8c`. `FUN_005712b0` rolls it (`FUN_0053e2f0`) and on success `FUN_0055ca20` computes `new = L + rand(0..(T-L) × 50 / 100)` (`DAT_006bb528` is GNPRTB `0x181d` = 6173); `FUN_004ee1d0` writes `+0x90 + (new - L)`; result 3 (`FUN_00521880`). `FUN_00576700` on failure sets result 2 and changes nothing. The mission is not hidden, runs 60 + rand(0..30) days, has `detection_phases = 1` and `repeats = 0` (MISSNSD, `0x58000042`), so it is subject to R11 while it runs.

**Teacher floor in play (arithmetic on the shipped values).** Luke's `+0x8c` starts at 50: below the teacher floor of 100 and below the discoverer floor of 80. Dagobah adds `50 × 60 / 100 = 30` (R15) to reach 80, which makes him a discoverer; each Vader meeting adds `max(1, (120 - L) × 25 / 100)`. Vader is a teacher from the start at 120, which also means a student must be at 119 or less: Palpatine (150) can never be Vader's student.

**Fixed ids, from a scan of every instruction for the six character ids** (`FindScalarReferences`; results classified by reading the callers): `FUN_00402cf0` and `FUN_004024d0` (AI object flags), `FUN_004025f0` (six main characters), `FUN_0058a9e0` and `FUN_0058ab00` (R11), `FUN_00560200` (R12), `FUN_00560a30` (R8), `FUN_0055f7b0`, `FUN_005438a0`, `FUN_00543690` and the `0x5426ed`-`0x54af72` run (story flags and chains: `story-missions.md`), `FUN_005180e0` (the four leaders in the game-end check: `FUN_005180e0.c:55-70`), `FUN_004c0fc0` and `FUN_004c30c0` (advisor scripts), `FUN_004f3e50`-`FUN_004f3f10`, `FUN_00506f50`-`FUN_005070d0` and the `0x0048xxxx` run (named-character getters and AI classes; no Force rule read there). hyp: the `0x0048xxxx` and `FUN_005425e0` users do nothing Force-specific; they were not read.

## Mod surface

What a mod adding Force users, teachers or Force-like content has to change in REBEXE (everything else is data):

1. **A new Force user is DAT-only.** Set `jedi_probability`, `is_known_jedi`, `jedi_level_base`/`variance` in MJCHARSD or MNCHARSD (R1 to R4, R5 to R7). To make a character the discoverer or a teacher also set `is_jedi_trainer`; they then need Force >= 80 (GNPRTB 2580) to discover and >= 100 (GNPRTB 2581) to teach.
2. **The two thresholds, the teaching gain and the skill bonus**: GNPRTB 2580, 2581, 6173 are data. The skill bonus `base × Force / 100` is literal code (`FUN_0053e940`, `FUN_0053e900` with the divisor `DAT_00661a88`) and needs a patch to change.
3. **Id-keyed rules need REBEXE patches**: the Luke/Leia gating of discovery (`FUN_00560a30`: two `cmp` against `0x31000241` and `0x32000242`); the Luke and Vader detection flagging (`FUN_0058a9e0`, `FUN_0058ab00`); the AI "Force >= 100" class bit (`FUN_00402cf0`); the meeting rewards for Luke and Leia from Vader and the Emperor (`FUN_00560200`, six literal ids); Luke's and Leia's Evade, the Luke flags and the Dagobah effect (class-specific). A mod with a new hero, a new mentor, or a second Dark Side teacher gets none of these.
4. **No Dark Side or Light Side attribute exists.** A mod that wants alignment-dependent behaviour must add the field and its readers; today the only difference between sides is the ids above and the owner bits.
5. **The AI will not use Jedi Training**: `FUN_00402720` must set a capability bit for `0x58000042` and the planner needs a consumer for it (`ai-mission-planning.md`); for a mod that needs Empire training for the computer player this is a code change, not data.
6. **Adding more than the shipped trainers** is safe in DAT, but the validator needs a student strictly below the teacher, so a mod whose teachers are weaker than 100 must lower GNPRTB 2581.
